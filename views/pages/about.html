About LinkLantern

LinkLantern is an open-source broken link checker you can use here or run on your own server.

It was built for the people who keep websites in good repair: developers, content editors, SEO specialists and agencies looking after dozens of client sites. The aim is a tool that's quick to use, honest about what it found, and considerate towards the servers it visits.

About the crawler

If you've seen {{token}} in your server logs, someone asked LinkLantern to check a website that links to yours, or your own site. The crawler identifies itself as:

{{userAgent}}

It only requests URLs that appear as links on the site being checked, obeys robots.txt and Crawl-delay, keeps to two parallel requests per host, and backs off if your server signals it's busy. To exclude it, add a robots.txt rule for User-agent: {{token}}. The FAQ has examples.
