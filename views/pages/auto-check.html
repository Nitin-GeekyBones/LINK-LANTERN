Auto check

Put your websites on a schedule. LinkLantern scans them daily, weekly or monthly and emails you a report, so new broken links get spotted within days rather than months.

What you get

  • Hands-off scanningPick a site and a frequency once. Scans run whether or not you're logged in.
  • Big sites welcomeScheduled scans check far more than the {{manualMaxLinks}}-link limit of a manual check, up to your monthly allowance.
  • Fine controlIgnore URL patterns, slow the crawl down, adjust timeouts, keep the scan inside one folder.
  • Quiet when all is wellOptionally skip the email when a scan finds nothing broken.
  • Slack and content checksPost each report to a Slack channel, and search every page for emails, phone numbers or your own phrases.
