Why broken links are worth fixing

A dead link is a promise your site can't keep. Visitors who hit a 404 tend to leave, and search engine crawlers that follow links into dead ends spend their budget on nothing and index less of what matters.

Links rot quietly: pages get renamed, partners move, images get deleted from a CMS. A regular check catches them before your visitors do.

  • Every reference, not just anchorsImages, srcset candidates, scripts, stylesheets, iframes, media, form targets, meta refresh and CSS url() values.
  • Redirect chains spelled outEach hop is listed with its status code, so you can link straight to the final address.
  • Polite by designObeys robots.txt and Crawl-delay, limits how hard it hits your server, and backs off when asked to.
  • Export everythingDownload any report as CSV for a spreadsheet, or JSON for your own tools.
