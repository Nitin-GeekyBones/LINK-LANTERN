Terms & privacy

The short version: check sites you have a reason to check, and we keep only what's needed to run the service.

This is a template for operators of a LinkLantern installation. Replace it with terms that fit your organisation and jurisdiction.

Using the service

Use LinkLantern to check websites you own, manage, or link to. Don't use it to put load on sites that haven't invited it, to get around access controls, or to probe for vulnerabilities. The crawler obeys robots.txt and limits its request rate, but you're responsible for the checks you start.

Results are provided as they are. A link reported as broken may work for other visitors, and the reverse; see the FAQ for common reasons.

What we store

  • Account details: your email address and a salted, hashed password. Never the password itself.
  • Reports: the URLs checked, their results and where they were found. Reports from checks run without an account are deleted after 7 days.
  • Schedules: the Auto check settings you create.
  • A session cookie to keep you logged in. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

Your IP address is held in memory only while a check you started is running, to enforce fair-use limits. It isn't written to reports.

Email

We only email the address on your account: reports you asked for, password resets and essential service notices.

Deleting your data

Delete individual reports from My reports, or your whole account from Account settings. Deletion is immediate and permanent.
