How a site check works

LinkLantern loads your starting page, collects every URL it references and tests each one. Pages on the same host are opened and searched for more links; links to other sites are tested but not explored further. A subdomain such as blog.example.com counts as a different site.

Each URL is tested once, however many times it appears, and the report lists every page it was found on. Addresses that differ only in their query string are treated as different pages.

Free checks stop after {{manualMaxLinks}} links. For bigger sites, Auto check handles many thousands.

Reading the results

Broken The link returned an error (4xx, 5xx) or could not be reached at all: timeout, unknown host, refused connection, redirect loop.

Redirected The link works but goes through one or more redirects. Updating it to the final address saves a round trip.

Not checked robots.txt asked us not to fetch it, or the scan was stopped first.
